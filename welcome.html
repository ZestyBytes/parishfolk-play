Welcome to the Parishfolk play-test

Thank you for helping! Parishfolk is a cosy idle life game set in your real town. You start at 18, pick a path, earn while you're away and pop back to collect your pay and make small choices.

1. Put it on your Home Screen first

It works like an app from your Home Screen. Do this before you start, because on iPhone the Home Screen version keeps its own save, separate from Safari.

iPhone (Safari):

  1. Open the link above in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Parishfolk from your Home Screen and start there.

Android (Chrome):

  1. Open the link above in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

It also works in any browser on a computer.

2. How to play

  • Collect your pay. Your pay jar fills while you're away, for up to 8 hours. Pop back and tap Collect.
  • Do today's tasks. Three quick ones a day: money, progress and wellbeing. Skipping a day is fine.
  • Answer life events. A boiler breaks, an old friend visits: pick what to do, now or later.
  • Look after yourself. If you never do anything for your wellbeing, it slowly slips and holds your career back.
  • Save for something. Pick a goal on the Life tab: a car, a year travelling, a narrowboat…
  • Explore your town on the Map tab.

3. Your privacy

  • Your location is only used once, to find your town. Your home in the game is a random street at least 500 m from where you really are.
  • Your game is saved on your phone. Nothing is sent to us unless you send us your log.
  • "The Bean Counter" café and the shop preview are made up, to ask what you think. Nothing can be bought.

4. Day 7 and day 14: send us your log

Around Monday 26 October and again around Monday 2 November:

  1. In Parishfolk, open Settings (the cog), then Save your play-test log.
  2. Answer three quick questions, if you like:
    What are you working towards?
    Anything confusing?
    Would you keep playing?
  3. Tap Save log file and send it back to us the same way you got this link.

The log has your game's history, not your name, your town or any streets.

Found a problem?

Tell us what happened and roughly when; a screenshot helps. If the game ever says it couldn't open your save, tap Save the damaged file and send it to us.
